## About this policy

This policy covers **NebulaStudio**, the YouTube Shorts automation tool at [nebula.nut.in.th](https://nebula.nut.in.th/login), run by Nut (nut.in.th). It explains what data NebulaStudio accesses when you sign in with Google, why, and how you can remove it. The general privacy policy for the nut.in.th website is [here](/en/privacy/).

NebulaStudio is a private tool. Only Google accounts that the owner has added can sign in.

## Google user data NebulaStudio accesses

When you sign in, Google asks you to grant these permissions:

- **Basic profile (openid, email, profile)**: your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture
- **YouTube upload (youtube.upload)**: permission to upload videos to the YouTube channel you choose
- **YouTube account (youtube.readonly)**: your channel name, your playlists, and the status of videos uploaded through NebulaStudio <!-- CONFIRM: if the app requests the full "youtube" scope instead, change this line and say exactly what it manages -->

NebulaStudio does not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google service.

## How NebulaStudio uses this data

- **Basic profile**: to sign you in, check that your account is on the allowed list, and show who is signed in
- **YouTube upload**: to upload the Shorts you prepare in NebulaStudio to your channel, only when you schedule or publish them
- **YouTube account**: to let you choose the channel and playlist, and to show whether each upload is processing, published or failed

NebulaStudio does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

## Limited Use

NebulaStudio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Storage and security

- OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored on NebulaStudio's server so that scheduled uploads can run while you are away. They are used only to call Google APIs for your own account. <!-- CONFIRM: where tokens are stored and whether they are encrypted at rest -->
- Video files and their titles, descriptions and schedules are stored on the server until they are uploaded, then kept for up to 30 days so you can see the history. <!-- CONFIRM: real retention period -->
- Connections to NebulaStudio and to Google use HTTPS.
- Only the owner has administrative access to the server.

## Sharing

NebulaStudio does not share Google user data with anyone, except:

- With **Google / YouTube**, when it uploads a video or reads your channel on your behalf
- When required by law

## Keeping and deleting your data

- Your data is kept while your account is active in NebulaStudio.
- To delete it, email [hello@nut.in.th](mailto:hello@nut.in.th). Your tokens, profile data and stored videos will be deleted within 30 days. <!-- CONFIRM: contact address and timing -->
- You can remove NebulaStudio's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). NebulaStudio then can no longer reach your Google account.
- Videos already published to YouTube stay on your channel until you remove them in YouTube.

## YouTube API Services

NebulaStudio uses YouTube API Services. By using NebulaStudio you agree to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's use of your data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Significant changes that affect how Google user data is used will be shown in NebulaStudio before they apply.
