## Overview

nut.in.th is a static portfolio site. It has no accounts, no data-collection forms and no advertising cookies. This policy explains the small amount of data that can arise when you visit, in line with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA).

## Data controller

The owner of nut.in.th is the data controller. Contact: [hello@nut.in.th](mailto:hello@nut.in.th)

## What is collected

- **Server logs** The hosting provider may automatically record technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time, for security and operations
- **Emails you send** If you get in touch by email, we receive your name, email address and message
- **Language preference** The site may remember your chosen language in your own browser (localStorage). This is not sent to us

The site does not use visitor analytics or tracking cookies. If that changes, this policy will be updated and consent requested where the law requires it.

## Purposes and legal basis

- Keeping the site secure and running (legitimate interest)
- Replying to your email (legitimate interest, or steps you asked for before entering a contract)

## Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data may be processed by our hosting and email providers acting as processors, or disclosed where required by law.

## Retention

Server logs are kept according to the hosting provider's rotation. Emails are kept only as long as needed for the conversation and deleted when no longer needed.

## Your rights

Under the PDPA you may request access to, a copy of, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, object to processing, and complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee. To exercise these rights, email [hello@nut.in.th](mailto:hello@nut.in.th).

## Third-party content

Links to other sites, such as GitHub, are covered by those sites' own privacy policies. Fonts and images on this site are served from our own server, so opening a page makes no requests to outside services.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The last-updated date is shown at the top of this page.
