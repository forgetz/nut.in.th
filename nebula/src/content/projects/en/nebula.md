## What it is

The third version of nut.in.th. The earlier PHP site was replaced with a static build so it can be hosted anywhere that accepts plain files.

## Notes

- Thai and English for every page, including the terms of service and privacy policy
- A gallery that picks up any image dropped into one folder and resizes it at build time
- One command builds the site and uploads it over FTP
